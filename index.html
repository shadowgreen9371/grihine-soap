Our Story

Made by the Women of Ambootia

High in the Darjeeling Hills, on the slopes of the Ambootia Tea Estate in Kurseong, a committee of local women came together with one idea — to turn the herbs, flowers and fruits growing around their homes into something the world would love.

Every Grihine bar is handcrafted in small batches: ingredients picked from hill gardens, blended over slow heat, poured into hand-carved moulds, and cured in clean mountain air. No factories. No chemicals. Just patient hands and pure intent.

When you buy a Grihine soap, you directly support the income, independence and craft of the women who made it.
