Privacy Policy

Plain words about your data — because trust is an ingredient too.

Last updated: 11 June 2026 · Framed with reference to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

The short version: this website collects no personal data. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers. Your shopping cart lives only in your own browser and is never sent to us. We only receive personal information when you choose to send it — over WhatsApp, email or Instagram — and we use it for one thing: getting soap to your door.

1. Who we are (Data Fiduciary)

Grihine Soap is operated by the Kurseong Ambootia Women's Committee, Ambootia Tea Estate, Kurseong, Darjeeling Hills, West Bengal — 734203, India. For anything in this policy, contact us at grihinesoap@gmail.com.

2. What this website does — and doesn't — collect

  • No cookies. We set none — not even "essential" ones.
  • No analytics or tracking. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no fingerprinting of any kind.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts and all assets are served from this site itself; opening a page here sends your browser data to no one else.
  • Cart stays on your device. The cart uses your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your phone or computer and you can clear it any time from your browser settings.
  • The order form sends nothing to us directly. Pressing "Send Order via WhatsApp" only opens WhatsApp on your device with a pre-typed message — nothing is transmitted unless you press send inside WhatsApp.

3. Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may temporarily log technical data such as IP addresses for security and abuse prevention; this is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have no access to those logs.

4. Personal data you choose to send us

When you message us on WhatsApp, email or Instagram to place an order, we receive what you send — typically your name, phone number, delivery address and order details.

  • Purpose: processing and delivering your order, replying to your enquiry, and keeping basic records of sales.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by voluntarily contacting us (Section 6, DPDP Act 2023).
  • Sharing: only with the courier service, and only your name, address and phone number — the minimum needed for delivery. We never sell, rent or trade your data, and we send no marketing messages without your explicit consent.
  • Retention: order details are kept only as long as needed for delivery, returns and our simple accounts, then deleted.

Messages exchanged on WhatsApp and Instagram are also subject to those platforms' own privacy policies (Meta Platforms). WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted in transit.

5. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

  • Access — ask what personal data of yours we hold.
  • Correction & erasure — ask us to fix or delete it.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as it was given.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint and get a response.
  • Nominate — appoint someone to exercise these rights for you.

To exercise any right, message us on WhatsApp or email grihinesoap@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We respond within 7 working days. If unsatisfied, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Our website and products are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from children; orders should be placed by adults.

If we ever add features that handle data differently (for example online payments), we will update this page first and change the "last updated" date above.
